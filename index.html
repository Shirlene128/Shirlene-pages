Science and Pseudoscience

This is the most interesting course I've taken so far. We look at unscientific claims — from horoscopes to paranormal phenomena — and try to explain or debunk them using scientific reasoning.

It's both fascinating and challenging. The course taught me to question what seems obvious, and to think more rigorously about the information I encounter every day.

About Time (2013)

Synopsis

Tim discovers he can travel in time and decides to use his gift to find love and improve his life. But as he learns, the real secret is not time travel — it's learning to live each ordinary day to the fullest.

Why I love it

It made me think about how I want to live: with more awareness, less rush.

Atonement (2007)

Synopsis

Set in 1930s England, a young girl's false accusation destroys the lives of her older sister and her lover. Spanning decades and war, the story becomes one of guilt, love, and the desperate attempt to atone.

Why I love it

The ending broke my heart. It stayed with me for days.

Welcome to the Jungle (2013)

Synopsis

A group of office workers is sent to a tropical island for a team-building retreat — but the survival instructor dies, and they're stranded with no real skills. Chaos, comedy, and unlikely friendships follow.

Why I love it

A fun, easy watch — silly but surprisingly warm at the end.

Harry Potter Series (2001–2011)

Synopsis

An orphaned boy discovers he's a wizard and enters Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where he makes lifelong friends, learns magic, and confronts the dark wizard who killed his parents.

Why I love it

It's more than fantasy — it's about friendship, courage, and growing up. A world I never want to leave.

The Hollow Cross (空洞的十字架)

Plot

A couple's young daughter is murdered. Years later, the killer is caught, but the real question becomes: what does justice actually mean? Is the death penalty the answer, or just another hollow cross to bear?

Why it stays with me

It's not just a mystery — it's a meditation on crime, punishment, redemption, and choice.

The Miracles of the Namiya General Store (解憂雜貨店)

Plot

Three young thieves hide in an abandoned grocery store and discover it has a magical mailbox — letters from people decades earlier seeking advice. As they reply, their own lives begin to change.

Why I love it

Warm, gentle, and unexpectedly moving. Every small story connects in a beautiful way.

Hope no Ito / 希望之線

Plot

A café becomes the meeting point for two strangers whose lives quietly intertwine. Told in Higashino's signature style, it's a gentle story about hope, connection, and the small threads that bind us.

Why I love it

Softer than his mysteries — and that's what makes it special.

Kinen no Sho / 祈念之書

Plot

A young man learns about his late father's hidden past through a mysterious tree where people come to pray and remember. A story about family, memory, and the things we carry.

Why I love it

Quiet and reflective — the kind of book that makes you think about your own family.

Masquerade Hotel (假面飯店)

Plot

A detective goes undercover as a hotel employee to catch a serial killer who leaves coded clues at the hotel. Behind the polished service lies a web of secrets.

Why I love it

A clever mix of mystery and human observation — you never quite know who to trust.

🇯🇵 Japan, September 2026

The Story

Early September, four friends — including me — decided on a whim to go to Japan. No months of planning, just a spontaneous "let's do it." We spent a week there, shopping non-stop, eating everything, laughing until we couldn't stand.

Even after returning to Hong Kong, it still feels like a dream. Every photo brings a smile back. It wasn't a perfect trip — it was a spontaneous one, and that made it unforgettable.

What I learned

Sometimes the best memories come from plans you never made.
